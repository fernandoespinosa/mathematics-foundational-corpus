# Dependency Roadmap

This is the working dependency order for the corpus. It is deliberately not purely chronological: later-discovered foundational ideas may appear early if they are prerequisite to understanding large portions of modern mathematics.

## Layer 0 — Mathematical language and foundations

- Logic: syntax, semantics, proof, models
- Naive and axiomatic set theory
- Relations, functions, equivalence relations, quotient constructions
- Countability and cardinality
- Cantor's theorem
- Choice, well-ordering, Zorn's lemma
- Compactness and completeness in first-order logic
- Gödel incompleteness
- Computability and the halting problem

## Layer 1 — Arithmetic and elementary structure

- Peano arithmetic and induction
- Euclidean algorithm
- Fundamental theorem of arithmetic
- Congruences
- Chinese remainder theorem
- Fermat/Euler theorem
- Polynomial arithmetic
- Fundamental theorem of algebra

## Layer 2 — Linear and abstract algebra

- Vector spaces, bases, dimension
- Rank-nullity
- Dual spaces
- Eigenvalues and canonical forms
- Spectral theorem
- Groups and quotient groups
- Lagrange and Sylow theorems
- Rings, ideals, quotient rings
- Fields and extensions
- Galois theory
- Modules and structure theorems

## Layer 3 — Analysis

- Completeness of the real numbers
- Limits and continuity
- Intermediate and extreme value theorems
- Compactness in Euclidean space
- Fundamental theorem of calculus
- Metric spaces and completeness
- Banach fixed-point theorem
- Measure and Lebesgue integration
- Monotone and dominated convergence
- Radon–Nikodym theorem

## Layer 4 — Topology

- Topological spaces
- Compactness and connectedness
- Product and quotient topology
- Tychonoff theorem
- Fundamental group
- Covering spaces
- Brouwer fixed-point theorem
- Invariance of domain

## Layer 5 — Functional analysis

- Normed and Banach spaces
- Hilbert spaces
- Hahn–Banach theorem
- Uniform boundedness principle
- Open mapping theorem
- Closed graph theorem
- Riesz representation
- Spectral theory of operators

## Layer 6 — Complex analysis

- Holomorphicity
- Cauchy integral theorem and formula
- Liouville theorem
- Residue theorem
- Argument principle
- Riemann mapping theorem

## Layer 7 — Smooth manifolds and differential geometry

- Smooth manifolds and tangent spaces
- Differential forms
- Stokes' theorem
- de Rham cohomology
- de Rham theorem
- Riemannian metrics
- Levi-Civita connection
- Curvature
- Gauss's Theorema Egregium
- Gauss–Bonnet theorem

## Layer 8 — Algebraic topology

- Homology
- Mayer–Vietoris
- Cohomology
- Cup products
- Hurewicz theorem
- Poincaré duality
- Characteristic classes

## Layer 9 — Commutative algebra

- Noetherian rings
- Hilbert basis theorem
- Localization
- Prime and maximal ideals
- Integral extensions
- Dimension theory
- Noether normalization

## Layer 10 — Algebraic geometry

- Affine algebraic sets
- **Hilbert's Nullstellensatz**
- Coordinate rings and the algebra–geometry dictionary
- Projective varieties
- Bézout's theorem
- Divisors
- Riemann–Roch
- Schemes and Spec
- Sheaves
- Sheaf cohomology
- Serre duality
- GAGA
- Grothendieck–Riemann–Roch

## Layer 11 — Lie theory and representation theory

- Lie groups and Lie algebras
- Exponential map
- Lie correspondence
- Semisimple Lie algebras
- Root systems and Dynkin diagrams
- Representation theory
- Maschke's theorem
- Schur's lemma
- Peter–Weyl theorem
- Weyl character formula

## Layer 12 — Number theory and arithmetic geometry

- Quadratic reciprocity
- Dirichlet's theorem
- Prime number theorem
- Dedekind domains
- Minkowski theorem
- Local fields
- Hasse–Minkowski theorem
- Class field theory / Artin reciprocity
- Elliptic curves
- Mordell–Weil theorem
- Faltings' theorem
- Modularity theorem

## Layer 13 — Probability and stochastic analysis

- Measure-theoretic probability
- Law of large numbers
- Central limit theorem
- Conditional expectation
- Martingales
- Kolmogorov extension theorem
- Brownian motion
- Itô calculus
- Feynman–Kac formula

## Layer 14 — Differential equations and dynamics

- Picard–Lindelöf theorem
- Sturm–Liouville theory
- Weak solutions
- Lax–Milgram theorem
- Distribution theory
- Stable manifold theorem
- Poincaré–Bendixson theorem
- KAM theorem

## Layer 15 — Category theory and homological algebra

- Categories, functors, natural transformations
- Universal properties
- Yoneda lemma
- Adjunctions
- Abelian categories
- Exact sequences
- Snake lemma
- Ext and Tor
- Derived functors
- Derived categories

## Layer 16 — Great synthesis theorems

These are best studied only after several earlier branches have converged.

- Riemann–Roch and its generalizations
- de Rham theorem
- Hodge theory
- Serre duality
- Grothendieck–Riemann–Roch
- Atiyah–Singer index theorem
- Poincaré conjecture / Ricci flow

## Layer 17 — Computation, combinatorics, and optimization

- Church–Turing thesis
- Halting problem
- Rice's theorem
- Cook–Levin theorem
- Ramsey theorem
- Hall's marriage theorem
- Max-flow min-cut
- Linear programming duality
- Farkas' lemma
- KKT conditions

---

## Guiding principle

The order should answer the question:

> **What must I already understand for this theorem to feel inevitable rather than magical?**

Individual chapters may therefore cross-reference earlier material rather than re-teach all prerequisites.
